ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Selected Controls

A.5.19 Information security in supplier relationships
Processes and procedures shall be defined and implemented to manage information security risks associated with the use of supplier products or services.

A.8.2 Privileged access rights
The allocation and use of privileged access rights shall be restricted and managed.

A.8.10 Information deletion
Information stored in information systems, devices or any other storage media shall be deleted when no longer required.
